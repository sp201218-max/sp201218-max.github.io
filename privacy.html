Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy covers the Google app "frank-postiz", a private tool used only by the owner of the Frank Checks The Maths channel to upload and schedule videos to that channel.

What data the app uses

When the owner connects their own YouTube account, Google gives the app an access token and a refresh token. The app uses them only to upload the owner's videos, set their titles, descriptions and privacy settings, and schedule them on the owner's channel (YouTube Data API scopes for uploading and managing the owner's own videos).

Where it is stored

The tokens and the videos are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a self-hosted copy of Postiz. Nothing is sent to any other server except Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising. No data from other people is collected.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which deletes its tokens' validity immediately.
